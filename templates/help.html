📚 Help & User Guide

Welcome to the PCS-APP Mouse Gene Expression Explorer

This database contains RNA-sequencing data from a study investigating the effects of perinatal choline supplementation on gene expression in App NL-G-F Alzheimer's disease mouse model.

🎯 Study Design

  • Genotypes: APP transgenic mice vs Wild-type (WT) controls
  • Diet: Control diet vs Choline-supplemented diet
  • Sex: Male and Female
  • Ages: 3, 6, 9, and 12 months
  • Brain Regions: Cortex and Hippocampus

🔧 How to Use Gene Search Page

  1. Click "Gene Expression Lookup" from the dashboard
  2. Enter a gene name (e.g., Bdnf, Apoe, Mapt)
  3. Choose filters (tissue, age, genotype, sex, diet) or leave as "All"
  4. Click "Run Gene Lookup"
  5. View expression results table with normalized counts

Popular genes to try: Bdnf, Grn, Itgax, Apoe, Mapt

📊 How to Use Differential Expression Analysis Page

  1. Click "Differential Expression Results" from the dashboard
  2. Select filters to define your comparison:
    • Tissue, Age, and other variables
    • Contrast (e.g., APP vs WT)
    • Direction (upregulated, downregulated, or both)
  3. Optionally adjust the FDR cutoff to control statistical significance
  4. Click "Run Query" to generate results
  5. Explore the output:
    • Ranked results table of differentially expressed genes
    • Key metrics: log2 fold change and FDR
    • Volcano plot to visualize significant genes

Tip: Start with broader filters and a standard FDR cutoff (0.05), then refine to focus on the most significant genes. Use the volcano plot to quickly identify strongly up- or down-regulated genes.

💡 Tips & Best Practices

  • Gene Names: Use mouse gene symbols (e.g., Bdnf not BDNF)
  • Filters: Start broad (All) then narrow down to specific conditions
  • No Results? Check spelling or try related gene names
  • Expression Values: Normalized RNA-seq counts (higher = more expression)
